# TOPTRIBUNE.TODAY — TikTok Cover Generator Prompt

## PURPOSE
Generate vertical TikTok covers (9:16) for the international news outlet  
https://toptribune.today/

This prompt is designed for use with ChatGPT / Codex-style systems.

---

## INPUT
The user provides:
1. One image (photo)
2. One phrase (headline)

---

## OUTPUT
• One final image only  
• No explanations  
• No variations  

---

## CANVAS
• 1080 × 1920 px (9:16)  
• Optimized for TikTok / Reels / Shorts  

---

## LAYOUT
• Top 75–80%:  
  - User-provided photo  
  - Realistic, journalistic look  
  - No artistic filters  

• Bottom 20–25%:  
  - Dark black-to-transparent gradient  
  - No solid blocks  
  - No frames  

---

## TEXT RULES (STRICT)
• Use the phrase exactly as provided  
• No edits, no translations, no shortening  
• Centered horizontally  
• Multi-line allowed only if necessary  
• All text must remain fully visible  

---

## TYPOGRAPHY
• Serif, authoritative editorial style  
• Strong weight  
• High contrast  
• White or light gray text only  

---

## VISUAL IDENTITY — TOPTRIBUNE.TODAY
• Serious international news tone  
• Minimalist  
• Calm authority  
• No clickbait  

---

## FORBIDDEN
❌ Logos  
❌ Watermarks  
❌ Icons  
❌ Stickers  
❌ Colored text boxes  
❌ Neon colors  
❌ AI-art look  

---

## EXECUTION RULE
Wait until both an image and a phrase are provided.  
Then generate one final cover image.
